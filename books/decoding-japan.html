Decoding Japan

History, Geography, Culture, Politics, and Economics — Six Lenses on the Indispensable Nation of Asia

What you will learn

  • The historical events that shaped modern Japan
  • The geographic and demographic realities of Japan
  • The cultural codes and social structures of Japan
  • The political system and international position of Japan
  • The economic strengths, weaknesses, and future of Japan

About this book

Understand Japan through six lenses: history, geography, culture, politics, economics and the future. Decoding Japan is written for readers who want to understand Japan in a structured way — not through fragments of news or travel writing, but through the underlying history, geography, culture, politics and economics that shape it today.

Each chapter builds on primary sources — government statistics, official disclosures and peer-reviewed research — to answer the questions that headline coverage tends to skip: how Japan came to be what it is, what forces shape its present, and where it is heading. The book is written to be accessible to business professionals, students and general readers alike, without oversimplifying the material.

Available on Kindle for smartphone, tablet and PC. A companion volume to the Decoding the World series.

Who this book is for

  • Journalists and analysts covering Japan
  • Anyone seeking a structured, in-depth understanding of Japan
