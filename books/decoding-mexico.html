Decoding Mexico

History, Geography, Culture, Politics, and Economics — Five Lenses on the Real Mexico

この本で分かること

  • The historical events that shaped modern Mexico
  • The geographic and demographic realities of Mexico
  • The cultural codes and social structures of Mexico
  • The political system and international position of Mexico
  • The economic strengths, weaknesses, and future of Mexico

本の紹介

From Aztec heritage to nearshoring: understand modern Mexico through five analytical lenses. Decoding Mexico is written for readers who want to understand Mexico in a structured way — not through fragments of news or travel writing, but through the underlying history, geography, culture, politics and economics that shape it today.

Each chapter builds on primary sources — government statistics, official disclosures and peer-reviewed research — to answer the questions that headline coverage tends to skip: how Mexico came to be what it is, what forces shape its present, and where it is heading. The book is written to be accessible to business professionals, students and general readers alike, without oversimplifying the material.

Available on Kindle for smartphone, tablet and PC. A companion volume to the Decoding the World series.
